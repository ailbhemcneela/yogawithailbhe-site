Privacy

Yoga with Ailbhe is run by Ailbhe McNeela in County Dublin, Ireland. This page explains what information I keep about you, why, and what you can ask me to do with it. If you have any questions, email hello@yogawithailbhe.ie.

What I collect

When you book a class or event, your booking goes through Setmore. I see your name, email address, phone number if you give it, which classes you've booked, and whether you've paid.

When you join my mailing list, your email address and name are kept by MailerLite, which I use to send occasional emails about classes and events. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

When you email me, I keep the conversation so I can reply and help with your booking.

When you visit this website, and only if you click Accept on the cookie banner, Google Analytics records how visitors find and use the site. This doesn't tell me who you are. If you click Decline, no analytics cookies are set.

How I use it

I use your information to run your bookings, take payment, get in touch about classes you've booked, and send the newsletter if you've signed up. I also keep booking, payment and newsletter records in a private, secure workspace on Google Cloud, so I can understand how my classes are doing and plan new ones. Only I and the person who helps me run that workspace can see it.

I never sell your information or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

How long I keep it

I keep booking and payment records for as long as I need them to run the business and meet Irish tax and accounting rules. If you'd like your details removed sooner, just ask and I'll delete anything I'm not legally required to keep.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of the information I hold about you, or ask me to correct it or delete it. Email hello@yogawithailbhe.ie and I'll get back to you within a month. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can contact the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
